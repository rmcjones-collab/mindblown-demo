How a brain unfolds

Depth is deliberate. Nothing is hidden behind menus; it is hidden behind distance.

  1. Lobes

    Zoomed out

    Colour-coded clusters of related pages. Switch any lobe off to clear the view; the layout settles around what is left. Press L to hide labels, or take a screenshot from the zoom controls.

  2. Neurons

    One per page

    Sized by how many notes a page holds. Click one for its panel: description, sections, links to neighbours and the sources behind it.

  3. Sub-neurons

    Past 0.85×

    Pages with a parent fold into it at lobe zoom, then unfold onto a ring around it as you move closer.

  4. Synapses

    Past 1.5×

    Every bullet becomes a particle orbiting its neuron. Read it in place, follow its wikilinks, or flip “Explode every neuron” to see them all.

  5. Sources

    Trace it back

    Citations thread back to sessions, repositories and web pages. Turn on shared-source threads to see which pages were learned together.

Build module

A brain that grows as you talk to it

  • Interview, not forms. It asks your name first, then follows up on what you said. Every question and category can be switched off, renamed, or written by you.
  • Your own synapses. Connect any two neurons with a label (“mother of”, “best friends”), notes and a strength of 1 to 3.
  • Neuron → brain. Grow a person or project into a brain of its own, still tied to yours. A header switch moves between brains; Explode shows the whole network.
  • Files included. Each category and neuron holds pictures, documents and links.
  • Nothing leaves the browser. The network lives in IndexedDB and saves to a single .mindblown.json you keep.

Point it at your own brain

MindBlown is a static site: an HTML shell, one renderer, one data file. The extractor reads a wiki folder and writes the dataset; the renderer never needs to know whose brain it is.

1

Describe the lobes

Edit lobes.json: a centre page, and for each lobe a list of substrings that pick its pages.

2

Extract the wiki

python3 build_data.py ~/wiki data.js reads frontmatter, bullets, wikilinks and citations into window.MINDBLOWN.

3

Open it

Serve the folder and open projects.html. Keep the data file private if the brain is.

About the data. Everything in the example brains is fictional: the cast, the places, the mail metadata and the code hosts. No real people, projects or messages appear. Build starts empty and stores only what you type, in your own browser.
