# Partnerships

MindBlown is built and owned by Rory Jones. The public demonstration site is free to
use. The application itself — the renderer, the lobe/neuron/synapse data model, the
build and extraction tooling, the MindBlown name and artwork — is proprietary and
**not for sale**.

## What is welcome

- Partnership and collaboration proposals (integrations, co-developed networks,
  hosting, research or education programmes).
- Commercial licensing enquiries for specific uses.
- Requests to publish a brain to the MindBlown AI Hub (see that repository's protocol).

## What is not on offer

- Acquisition of the application or its intellectual property.
- Permission to copy, fork, re-skin or redistribute the code or its data model
  outside the terms in `LICENSE.md`.

## How to get in touch

Open a GitHub issue on this repository titled **Partnership: <your organisation>**
with a short description of what you have in mind. Please do not include
confidential information in the issue; a private channel will be arranged for
anything that needs one. Any discussion is non-binding until a written agreement
is signed by the owner.
